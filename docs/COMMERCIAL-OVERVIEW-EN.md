# DocuFlow Desktop – License and Privacy Overview (Draft)

DocuFlow is an offline desktop tool for customer records, business data and editable document templates. It can export PDFs. It is not accounting or invoicing software, is not connected to tax authorities, and does not provide electronic signatures or legal validation.

## License model
A one-time purchase grants perpetual use of the purchased edition. The Professional license is verified locally from an Ed25519-signed license file. It supports unlimited installations on computers owned or controlled by the named licensee, with no device activation and no recurring subscription. The license is not transferable or resellable unless the publisher agrees in writing or mandatory law provides otherwise.

Fully offline, unlimited-device licensing has a clear trade-off: the publisher cannot remotely revoke a license, and copying a license file cannot be technically prevented in every case. A digital signature verifies the issuer; it is not unbreakable DRM.

## Data and privacy
Customer, document, line-item, template and company-profile data are stored locally in an SQLite database. The company logo is stored in that database and included in database backups. The current client has no built-in cloud sync, analytics or advertising tracker. The database is not encrypted by the application; users should protect the device with OS access controls and full-disk encryption and keep secure backups.

Generated documents are editable business drafts and templates. They are not automatically legally valid, tax compliant, signed or officially filed. Users are responsible for reviewing content and obtaining professional advice where required.
